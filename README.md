1. Introduction

4. Process Planning
The transmission shaft is manufactured mainly using CNC turning and NC/conventional machining operations. Grinding is not considered in this process. Required dimensional accuracy and surface finish are obtained through rough and finish turning.

Manufacturing Sequence
1. Raw Material Cutting
The EN8 round bar is cut to the required length using a band saw machine.

2. Facing and Centering
The workpiece is mounted on the CNC turning centre. Facing and centering operations are performed.

3. Rough Turning
Excess material is removed to bring the shaft close to the required dimensions.

4. Step and Profile Turning
Different shaft diameters, steps, shoulders, and required profiles are produced.

5. Grooving and Chamfering
Required grooves are machined and sharp edges are removed by chamfering.

6. Drilling / Tapping
Drilling or tapping is carried out wherever required according to the shaft design.

7. Finish Turning
Final dimensions and surface finish are achieved by precision finish turning.

8. Deburring
Burrs and sharp edges produced during machining are removed.

9. Inspection
The finished component is inspected for dimensional accuracy, length, diameter, runout, and surface finish.

Process Flow
Raw Material → Cutting → Facing & Centering → Rough Turning → Step/Profile Turning → Grooving & Chamfering → Drilling/Tapping → Finish Turning → Deburring → Inspection → Finished Product
